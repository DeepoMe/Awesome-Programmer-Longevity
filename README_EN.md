## Sample Entries (full)

### 004 · The 2 PM Caffeine Cutoff 🟢

**Practice**: No caffeine after 2 PM — coffee, strong tea, cola, energy drinks, some painkillers included.

**Mechanism**: Caffeine's half-life is ~5 h (range 3–7 h). A 2 PM cup still leaves ~50% in your blood at 7 PM; slow metabolizers carry more than half past midnight. Caffeine antagonizes adenosine receptors — it mutes the alarm, it doesn't put out the fire — and degrades deep-sleep architecture even when you "fall asleep fine."

**Evidence**: RCT — caffeine 6 h before bed still reduced objective sleep time while subjects felt unaffected (Drake 2013, PMID 24235903).

**Boundary**: Fast CYP1A2 metabolizers are not exempt; pregnancy extends the half-life beyond 15 h.

### 012 · 10 Minutes of Meditation 🟢

**Practice**: Daily 10 min; attention on the breath, count 1–10 and restart. Mind wandering is not failure — the *returning* is the training.

**Mechanism & evidence**: Meta-analysis of meditation programs for stress and well-being (Goyal 2014, PMID 24395196); workplace mindfulness RCT meta (PMID 30714811); meditation correlates with higher telomerase activity (meta, PMID 24636500). Molecular-layer inflammation signals exist but clinical marker effects are small — stated honestly.

### 018 · Memory via DNA Break-and-Repair 🟡

**Mechanism**: Landmark chain — neuronal activity induces TOP2B-mediated DNA double-strand breaks that drive early-response gene expression (*Cell* 2015, PMID 26052046); NPAS4-NuA4 couples synaptic activity to DNA repair (*Nature* 2023, PMID 36792830); memory assemblies form through DNA-sensing TLR9 (*Nature* 2024, PMID 38538785); in Alzheimer's brains, these *adaptive* breaks are lost. Your brain breaks its own DNA to write new memories — and the disease is failing to reconnect.
